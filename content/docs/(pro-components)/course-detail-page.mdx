## Introduction

**Course Detail Page** includes:

- **Header**: Back navigation, title, progress, and mobile curriculum drawer.
- **Video area**: Playback controls and lesson chrome.
- **Details**: Metadata, description, and tags.
- **Instructor**: Profile card with stats.
- **Curriculum**: Sticky sidebar on large screens (drawer on small screens).

Uses Button, Card, Drawer, Progress, ScrollArea, and Avatar.
