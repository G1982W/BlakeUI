## Introduction

Charts Group 8 includes:

- **KPI cards**: Three occupancy tiles with delta badge, capacity bar, and available-room counts.
- **Revenue chart**: Bar chart with a metric switcher, animated headline value, and peak / average / YTD readouts.
- **Activity panel**: Searchable patient feed grouped by Admissions, In-Patient, and Discharges with avatars and status badges.
- **Responsive layout**: Two-column on wide containers, stacked on narrow ones.

Uses Avatar, Badge, Card, Chart, ScrollArea, and Select.

## Usage

```tsx
import { ChartGroup9 } from "@/components/ui/chart-group-9";

<ChartGroup9 />;
```

Replace `roomCapacityStats` to drive the KPI cards, `salesMetricData` (keyed by `netRevenue` / `roomRevenue` / `platformRevenue`) to drive the bar chart, and `ADMISSIONS` / `IN_PATIENT` / `DISCHARGES` to populate the activity feed. The feed search filters across guest name, room, source, attribution, and status.
